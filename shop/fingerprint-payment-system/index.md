## What it does

You place your finger on the device. It checks your balance on the website, moves the money to the vendor, and both of you get an SMS with the amount and the new balance.

## What you get

- **ESP32 firmware** written in Arduino
- **Website and API** in PHP and MySQL: users page, admin page and the endpoints the device calls
- **Two of my Arduino libraries:** DatabaseOnSD and SetTimeout_G
- **Proteus schematic and PCB project**, plus a printable PCB layout
- **SolidWorks enclosure model** and 2D drawing
- **Photos, block diagram, parts list and setup guide**
{: .included}

## Hardware you will need

ESP32, FPM10A fingerprint sensor, SIM800L GSM module, 20x4 LCD, 4x4 keypad, DS3231 clock and an SD card module.
